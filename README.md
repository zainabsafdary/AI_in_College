# AI_in_College
Analysis of how AI use relates to human capital development among college students.

I used original survey data from 94 college students to examine how the frequency of AI use relates to learning and career readiness. The analysis:

- measures students' academic impact and work readiness
- compares human capital outcomes across different levels of AI use
- examines the relationship between AI-use frequency and students' perceived development of skills and preparedness for work

More frequent AI use was associated with stronger reported learning outcomes and greater work readiness, suggesting that AI use may complement human capital development among students.

The analysis was completed in **R**, using `tidyverse`, `ggplot2`, and statistical modeling.

The full analysis and visualization code is available in the project repository.

Data: Original survey data collected from 94 college students.
